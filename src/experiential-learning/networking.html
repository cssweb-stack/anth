Experiential Learning: Networking

Joining a professional society is a great way for students to network within their field and to build a stronger CV. Most professional groups offer student membership discounts and may provide full or partial funding opportunities for students to attend and/or present at conferences.

Professional Organizations

  • Society for Hawaiian Archaeology (SHA)
  • Society for America Archaeology (SAA)
  • American Anthropological Association (AAA)
  • American Association of Physical Anthropologists (AAPA)
