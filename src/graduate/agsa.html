Anthropology Graduate Student Association (AGSA)

AGSA is a volunteer organization composed of graduate students in the Department of Anthropology within the College of Social Sciences at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa.

Goals

AGSA has two primary goals:

  1. To support graduate student research, projects, and outreach activities.
  2. To assist in the facilitation of faculty-graduate student communication and be an interface between department administration and the graduate student body.

Activities and Campus Events

  • Interfaces with the Graduate Student Organization (GSO) to represent the interests of graduate students across campus.
  • Organizes events such as professional development workshops, an annual graduate student research symposium, community service projects, and occasional social events.

Officer Elections

Officer elections for AGSA officer positions take place early in the fall semester. Any graduate student in the department is eligible to run for these positions.
