Graduate Applications & Admissions

The admissions process is highly competitive. Prospective MA or PhD students are encouraged to familiarize themselves with our faculty. We welcome applications from individuals whose academic interests match our expertise.

  • The application deadline for Fall admission is January 15 (Fall admissions only).
  • Students must complete all application requirements below.

Application Requirements

Applicants must meet the application requirements of both the UH Mānoa Graduate Division and the Department of Anthropology.

UH Mānoa Graduate Division

Prospective students must:

  • Complete a Graduate Division online application.
  • Pay the application fee.

Please direct all questions concerning the application and associated requirements to Graduate Division.

Department of Anthropology

Prospective students must:

  • Complete a Department of Anthropology graduate program application.
  • GRE scores are not required at this time.
    • Applicants with valid scores may submit them in their application.
    • However, in lieu of the GRE, a writing sample is now required for both MA and PhD applicants.
  • All non-native English speakers are required to take the TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo, or Cambridge English scale, unless they have already received a degree from a college or university in an English speaking country.
    • See the minimum English proficiency score requirements.
  • Three letters of recommendation are required.
    • Applicants must direct their recommendation writers to complete a Department of Anthropology letter of recommendation form.

Please direct all questions concerning the application and associated requirements to anthprog@hawaii.edu.
