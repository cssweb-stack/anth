Lambda Alpha

Lambda Alpha National Anthropology Honor Society was established in 1968 to promote the study of anthropology by acknowledging and recognizing students for their exemplary contributions and achievements through society membership. Beta of Hawaiʻi, the department's chapter, was established in the spring of 2009. Lambda Alpha rewards academic excellence through the annual awarding of student scholarships, research grants, and student paper prizes.

Undergraduate Student Membership Requirements

The minimum requirements to become a lifetime member of Beta of Hawaiʻi for undergraduate students (majors or minors):

  • Must have completed 12 semester hours of coursework in anthropology
  • Have a minimum GPA of 3.00 in anthropology
  • Have a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.5

Graduate Student Membership Requirements

The minimum requirements to become a lifetime member of Beta of Hawaiʻi for graduate students:

  • Have been officially admitted to the anthropology graduate program
  • Have a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.25

Cost

A lifetime membership fee of $25.00, in the form of a check made out to "Lambda Alpha Honor Society", must be submitted to the department upon membership approval.

Graduation

Email anthprog@hawaii.edu at least a month prior to your graduation to have an honors designation associated with your name on the commencement program. Lambda Alpha stoles and pins should be ordered at least a month prior to graduation.
