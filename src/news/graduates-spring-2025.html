Dear Anthropology Community,

The Department of Anthropology is thrilled to celebrate the achievements of our remarkable Spring 2024 graduates! We are immensely proud of their dedication, intellectual curiosity, and the insightful contributions they have made to our department.

Each of these graduates has demonstrated a deep commitment to anthropological inquiry, exploring diverse facets of human culture and history. We are confident that they will go on to make significant contributions to their communities and fields.

As they embark on their next adventures, we extend our warmest wishes for success and fulfillment. We encourage them to stay connected with the Department of Anthropology and to continue to apply their anthropological knowledge to create positive change.

Eirik Saethre
Chair, Department of Anthropology, UH Mānoa
