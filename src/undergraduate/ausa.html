Anthropology Undergraduate Student Association (AUSA)

AUSA is a student-led organization for undergraduate majors and non-majors interested in anthropology.

Opportunities

AUSA provides opportunities to:

  • Learn more about anthropology.
  • Participate in networking events for students with interests in anthropology.
  • Participate in community service events.
  • Find out about field school, grants, fellowships, and research opportunities of interest to anthropology students.

Students interested in joining AUSA should contact ausa@hawaii.edu.
