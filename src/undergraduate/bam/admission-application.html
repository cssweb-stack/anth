BAM Admission & Application

Admission to the Anthropology BAM pathway requires advance planning and completion of both university and departmental application requirements.

Admission Requirements

Students must:

  • Be a declared BA Anthropology major at UH Mānoa.
  • Have a minimum GPA of 3.0.
  • Earn a grade of B (not B-) or higher in ANTH 300: Contemporary Social Issues during the fall semester of their junior year.
    • Junior standing means 60–89.9 earned credits, excluding in-progress credits.
  • Apply no later than their junior year (see Frequently Asked Questions).

How to Apply

Students submit the Graduate Admissions Application and fee as well as all required anthropology program admission materials by the deadline below to be considered for the Fall admission of the same year.

  • Interested students must schedule a meeting with the major advisor (see Anthropology Undergraduate Advising).
  • Students must submit applications by January 15.
  • Follow the Graduate Applications & Admissions procedures.
    • Application materials are required by both UH Mānoa Graduate Division and the Department of Anthropology.
