SLOs & ILOs

The Anthropology BA identifies the following student learning outcomes and institutional learning objectives.

  1. Think anthropologically and cross-culturally about issues of human evolution and diversity.
  2. Know and use methods that anthropologists employ across sub-disciplines and specializations.
  3. Write and speak about cultural and biological patterning and evolution.
  4. Understand the physical, biological, and cultural dimensions of the Hawaiʻi, Pacific, and Asian regions.
