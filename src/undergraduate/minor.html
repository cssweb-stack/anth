Minor in Anthropology

The minor in anthropology at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa provides an option to gain a firm grounding in the discipline without fulfilling the full range of requirements of the major. Students majoring in many fields may find that a minor in anthropology will both complement and enhance their understanding of their major. Students can take any of the department’s upper-division courses to fulfill the minor requirements, offering great flexibility to choose courses that fit their academic interests.

Requirements

Students pursuing a minor in anthropology must complete 15 credits of upper-division (300+) anthropology courses.

Required Courses

  • One upper division (300+) Theory course
  • One upper division (300+) Method course

Courses must be completed with a grade of C (not C-) or better and cannot be used to fulfill any additional major, minor, certificate, or *HSL culture sub requirements.

* This applies to students whose primary major is within the College of Social Sciences (CSS); students whose primary major is outside CSS should consult their major or college advisor as some UH colleges do allow double-dipping of HSL culture courses.
