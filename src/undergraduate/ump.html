Undergraduate Mentoring Program (UMP)

This program brings together graduate and undergraduate students through mentoring. Graduate mentors gain teaching experience and help undergraduates explore anthropology, while undergraduate mentees gain insights into the discipline and graduate student life.

Mentoring Activities

In general, graduate mentors and undergraduate mentees meet once a week. Mentoring activities may include:

  • Assistance and guidance with coursework.
  • Assistance and guidance with honors theses.
  • Discussions of how to apply to graduate school.
  • Discussions of graduate student research.
  • Guided attendance of events such as departmental colloquia.
  • Guided attendance of dissertation defenses.

At the end of each semester, mentors and mentees are required to submit a one-page assessment of their experiences.

How to Participate

Anthropology majors and minors are invited to participate at the beginning of each semester through the undergraduate email list.
